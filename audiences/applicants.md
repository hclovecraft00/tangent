**Subject:** Four weeks to build something that pays for itself

---

I wanted to make sure you know about a programme I think might be right for you, or for someone you know.

Tangent is a free, not-for-profit incubator in Singapore for people who want to build something of their own that pays its way quickly. That can be a business, a social enterprise, a not-for-profit or a creative practice, in any sector. What we care about is whether you have a credible path to cash flow, fast.

**What we look for:**
- **A real problem in an existing market**, for a specific group of people, using technology that already works. Not a speculative new market, and not a technology that still has to be invented.
- **A path to self-sustaining early.** Customers paying, a government contract, grants or philanthropic funding, plus reasoned choices about who to serve first, what to charge, and what to build now versus later.
- **Informed by AI.** You don't have to build AI products, but your idea should take AI into account.

More on this at https://tangent.org.sg/wishlist.

**What you get:** a four-week, in-person sprint. Each team gets:
- A discretionary tooling budget of S$800, plus S$200 per founder (for up to two founders)
- Access to AI tools and a place to work in person
- People with industry experience to talk your problem through with
- Financial support, if you couldn't otherwise do four weeks in person

There's no fixed curriculum and no mandatory assignments. You decide what you need to figure out, and we help you do it. At the end of the four weeks you share back what you did, and that's the only obligation. Tangent takes no equity and charges no fees, so you leave owing nothing.

It works: four teams from our first cohort had contracted revenue by the end of their four weeks.

You don't need grades, a technical background or a polished pitch to apply. Teams can have up to three people, and cohorts are timed around university term breaks.

Details, dates and the application are at https://tangent.org.sg.
