**Subject:** A four-week programme for building something of your own

---

I wanted to make sure you know about a programme I think might be right for you, or for someone you know.

Tangent is a free, not-for-profit incubator in Singapore for people who want to build something of their own: a business, a social enterprise, a not-for-profit, or a creative practice that could eventually make them a good living. We accept applicants aged 18 to 35, and we're especially keen on 18 to 22 year olds.

It's a four-week, in-person sprint. Each team gets:
- A discretionary tooling budget of S$800, plus S$200 per founder (for up to two founders)
- Access to AI tools and a place to work in person
- Guides from industry, matched to what your team actually needs
- Financial support, if you couldn't otherwise do four weeks in person

There's no fixed curriculum and no mandatory assignments. You decide what you need to figure out, and we help you do it. At the end of the four weeks you share back what you did. That's the only obligation. Tangent takes no equity, and you leave owing nothing.

Tangent isn't a venture capital incubator. We back people who want to solve a real problem for real customers and get to sustainable revenue fast, even if that means staying small. Four teams from our first cohort had contracted revenue by the end of their four weeks.

We select on motivation and self-direction, not grades or credentials. You don't need to be technical, and you don't need a polished idea or pitch. We're especially interested in applicants from polytechnics and ITE. Cohorts are timed to fit IHL term breaks.

Details, dates and the application are at https://tangent.org.sg. To see what we look for, read https://tangent.org.sg/wishlist.
