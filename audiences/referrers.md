**Subject:** Know a young person who's already building something?

---

I wanted to make sure you know about Tangent, and to ask for your help finding the right people for it.

Tangent (https://tangent.org.sg) is a free, not-for-profit incubator in Singapore for underserved potential entrepreneurs. We accept applicants aged 18 to 35, and we're especially keen on 18 to 22 year olds. It's a four-week, in-person sprint. Each team gets a discretionary tooling budget, access to AI tools, a place to work, and guides from industry matched to what they need. Financial support is available for anyone who couldn't otherwise take four weeks out. There's no curriculum and no assignments. The only obligation is to share back what they did at the end, and Tangent takes no equity.

**Who we're looking for.** The right person often isn't the top student. They're already building things without being asked to. They have a problem they keep coming back to, they're self-directed, and they may be restless in the conventional system. They don't need to be technical, and their idea doesn't need to be a company in the usual sense: social enterprises, not-for-profits and creative practices are all welcome. We're especially interested in polytechnic and ITE students.

**Why it works.** Tangent isn't a VC incubator. We back people who want to solve real problems in existing markets and get to sustainable revenue fast, rather than chase a unicorn. Our first cohort (17 participants, 10 projects) ran in July and August 2026, and four of its teams had contracted revenue by the end of the four weeks.

How you can help:
1. **Refer someone.** If one or two young people come to mind, send them to https://tangent.org.sg or refer them directly to us.
2. **Pass this on.** Forward it to colleagues or friends who work closely with young people in Singapore.
3. **Be a guide.** Spend two hours, once, with a team that's stuck on something you know about. There's nothing to prepare. More at https://tangent.org.sg/guide.

*If you want to know who's behind Tangent:* Tangent is run by Dr Vaughn Tan (executive director) and Harper Chew (programme associate). Vaughn is a Fellow at Singapore's Centre for Strategic Futures, ex-Google, and an honorary associate professor in strategy and entrepreneurship at UCL (https://vaughntan.org). Tangent is funded by private philanthropy through the Majurity Trust.
