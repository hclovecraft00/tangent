**Subject:** Drop by Tangent?

---

I'm writing to invite you to drop by Tangent while a cohort is running.

Tangent is a free, not-for-profit incubator in Singapore. Each cohort is a four-week, in-person sprint for founders working on real problems in existing markets, who are trying to get to sustainable cash flow fast.

There's no formal agenda and nothing to prepare. Come by the space whenever suits you, for as long as suits you. Founders who are stuck on something you know about will come and talk to you. What makes a visit valuable is real industry experience, such as having built or sold something in a particular sector, run operations in it, or been on the buying side of the decision a team is trying to understand.

If a conversation turns into something more, and you want to keep helping a team, you're welcome to. There's no obligation.

If you're open to it, reply and I'll send you the cohort dates and location. More at https://tangent.org.sg/guide. And if you know someone who'd enjoy this, please pass it on.
