**Subject:** Two hours with a young founder?

---

I'm writing to ask whether you'd spend two hours with a young founder at Tangent.

Tangent is a free, not-for-profit incubator in Singapore. Each cohort is a four-week, in-person sprint for founders building businesses that solve real problems in existing markets, aiming for sustainable revenue fast rather than unicorn scale.

Guides come in for a single, unstructured session with a team working on something specific and stuck on something specific. There's nothing to prepare and no lecture to give: the team brings the problem. What makes a guide valuable is real industry experience, such as having built or sold something in a particular sector, run operations in it, or been on the buying side of the decision the team is trying to understand.

We match guides to teams by what each team actually needs, rather than keeping a general pool. If you want to keep going with a team afterwards, you're welcome to, but there's no obligation.

If you're open to it, start at https://tangent.org.sg/guide. If you know someone in your organisation who'd be good at this, please pass this on.
